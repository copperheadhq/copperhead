# Mechanical and Manufacturing

## Requirements

### Board envelope

Given the PCB layout is complete, when its outline is measured, then it shall be no larger than 30 mm by 20 mm and include two through-hole M3 mounting holes at 24 mm center-to-center pitch.

### Manufacturability and cost

Given parts and stackup are selected, when the board is reviewed, then it shall use a standard JLCPCB-compatible 2-layer, 1 oz build with no controlled impedance, no package finer than 0603, no BGA, no QFN with exposed thermal pad, and a target quantity-100 BOM cost below US$3.
