# 3.3V regulation
## Requirements
The buck SHALL be rated >=600mA and maintain nominal 3.3V within 3.0–3.6V. All allocations, derived sums and ASSUMED >=75% efficiency floor in docs/SPEC.md Budgets SHALL remain active.
### Scenario: Burst load
- **Given** 4.75–5.25V input and startup/radio/display loads
- **When** performance is evaluated
- **Then** MCU <=150mA continuous/400mA transient, OLED <=50mA, heater-off sensor <=2mA and other rail loads <=20mA SHALL be checked against actual modes and limits; preliminary sums are 222mA continuous/472mA peak.
### Scenario: Input margin
- **Given** 472mA at 3.3V, 4.75V input and ASSUMED 75% efficiency
- **When** input current is reconciled
- **Then** the 437.2mA conversion estimate and about 12.8mA remaining margin SHALL include protection losses, quiescent/leakage, tolerances, thermal conditions and inrush before acceptance; any contradiction SHALL be reported without reducing requirements.
