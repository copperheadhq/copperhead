# RF and mechanics
## Requirements
Board SHALL be <=45x40mm, exactly two copper layers, nominal 1.00mm thick, with >=0.25mm hole-to-copper clearance and unchanged exact pad/drill geometry. Ambient SHALL be 0–40C non-condensing indoors.
### Scenario: Sensor placement
- **Given** MCU/regulator heat sources
- **When** draft layout is prepared
- **Then** sensor >=10mm separation SHALL be pursued where practicable and any conflict reported explicitly.
### Scenario: Antenna placement
- **Given** a PCB-antenna module at a board edge, preferably extending beyond the base board
- **When** layout is checked
- **Then** manufacturer keepout SHALL exclude copper/traces/components on both layers; additional 15mm in-plane target remains ASSUMED until hardware guidelines and actual outline are reconciled.
### Scenario: Mechanical acceptance
- **Given** GCT standard 1.00mm stakes and proposed assembly
- **When** fit is assessed
- **Then** shell/module/cable/enclosure fit SHALL be checked and draft status retained; DRC errors SHALL NOT be ignored.
