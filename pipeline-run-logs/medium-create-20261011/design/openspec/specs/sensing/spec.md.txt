# Temperature/humidity sensing
## Requirements
SHT40-AD1B-R2 SHALL remain a candidate pending ordering/address/current verification. Shared I2C SHALL run at 100kHz with compatible 3.3V pullups and verified parallel resistance/capacitance and address compatibility. Sampling SHALL be 1Hz, heater disabled, within <=2mA allocation.
### Scenario: Valid measurement
- **Given** a responding SHT40 and valid CRC
- **When** the 1Hz measurement is completed
- **Then** real temperature/humidity SHALL be supplied for display with heater off.
### Scenario: Sensor failure
- **Given** a bus or CRC error
- **When** a measurement fails
- **Then** errors SHALL be displayed/reported; ASSUMED initial behavior is invalid reading, explicit display error, UART logging and retry at next scheduled sample.
