## Ordered bring-up for a future completed prototype

Do not power or order the present draft as a functional prototype. First complete/review routing and the order gates below. Each step requires the prior step to pass; log failures and stop for unexplained current, rail excursions, heating, reversed cables or reset instability. Use a DMM, oscilloscope with short local ground connection, input current capture and I2C/UART logic analyzer. Bench power must enter only through J1 as a qualified5V USB-C source; never inject at J2,J3 or3V3, bypass U3 or apply PD/higher voltage. Instrument burden and cable loss must still leave4.75–5.25V at the receptacle. Rationale: isolate faults in power-to-interface order without changing the power architecture.

1. **Unpowered inspection and first meter checks.** Disconnect USB,UART and OLED; verify discharged rails before resistance mode. Inspect exact MPN/orientation, D1 cathode, U2/U3 exposed-pad soldering, U4 placement, J1 stakes and assembly bridges. Meter GND continuity from J1 grounds and each shell stake to local capacitor ground pads and J2.1/J3.1. Then measure VBUS,5V_PROT and3V3 resistance to GND and rail-to-rail isolation, accounting for charging capacitors and semiconductor paths; an unexplained persistent short blocks power. Verify intended endpoints, not just net labels, because the present draft leaves pads isolated. Verify each separate CC Rd path around5.1k and no direct CC1/CC2 short; compare readings with circuit loading and resistor tolerance rather than invent a universal rail-resistance threshold. Rationale: catch assembly and missing-return faults before applying energy.
2. **Initial power with peripherals disconnected.** Hold SW1 RESET to keep MCU_EN low, connect a documented adequate5V source through J1 and instrument its current. Set fixture protection conservatively for this configuration; record its setting and any limiting, which invalidates an unrestricted startup measurement. Meter VBUS at C1.1 first,5V_PROT at C2.1 second,then3V3 at C3.1 and local C4.1/C7.1/C8.1/J3.2. Scope startup current,5V_PROT,3V3,BUCK_EN and MCU_EN across cold starts and rapid disconnect/reconnect. Nominal C10 ramp and R5/C11 delay are predictions only; PG can float when U2 is off. Stop if the established rail or input limits fail. The3.0–3.6V operating envelope applies after rail rise and during supported operation, not a claim that an unpowered rail stays above3V. Rationale: qualify power and reset before boot/display loads obscure the cause.
3. **Reset, boot straps and buffered UART.** Verify GPIO2/8/9 at R7.2/R8.2/R9.2 and SW1/SW2 behavior. SPI boot requires GPIO2 high and GPIO9 high; joint download requires2 high,8 high,9 low. Hold BOOT through RESET release and keep strap levels for>=3ms after EN rises. Scope EN/rail sequencing rather than infer reset reliability from a DMM. After checking a powered3.3V adapter's levels, connect adapterGND to J2.1,adapterRX to J2.2,adapterTX to J2.3,with no power lead. Observe U5 paths and115200 logs, enter manual download and flash only in later authorized physical work. Test target-off/adapter-on leakage and target-on/adapter-off loading under controlled conditions; do not bypass U5 or assume Ioff eliminates every injection path. Rationale: establish safe programming and boot behavior before application testing.
4. **Sensor alone.** Keep OLED disconnected. Confirm bus idle near3V3,100kHz clock, U4 address0x44 and suffix. Capture command0xFD,10ms wait and six-byte reply; verify both CRCs and converted values against controlled reference conditions. Record valid readings and UART timestamps at1Hz, and verify no heater command. No accuracy threshold is invented; establish a separate explicit acceptance target before evaluating accuracy. Rationale: prove acquisition independently of display loading.
5. **Verify and attach the external OLED with power off.** Confirm actual Adafruit326 revision/controller,address,supply/reset/header and cable pin mapping; host J3 order is not a vendor-header-order claim. Verify pullups to3V3 only,external set>=4.7k per line or removed/documented,total bus capacitance<=200pF. Recheck resistance and cable continuity,then power through J1 and meter module supply before running its interface. Confirm configured0x3C without collision with0x44,reset pulse and128x64 rendering at2Hz. Measure OLED current including startup/content/brightness against50mA; contrast0x20 alone is no proof. Scope rise time<=1us and sink levels with module and cable installed. Rationale: avoid reversed supply,5V pullups and unqualified shared-bus loads.
6. **Exercise failures and timing.** Use a controlled bus fixture or separately reviewed test firmware to corrupt temperature CRC and humidity CRC independently,inject NACK/timeouts and hold a bus line low; record fixture loading and rebuild any changed firmware. Verify invalid samples suppress numeric fields on a reachable display,UART reports errors and acquisition retries on the next scheduled sample. Test OLED disconnect and mid-frame failure: code returns on the first failed page and retries initialization; the physical panel may retain stale/partial pixels until a successful refresh. An unreachable panel cannot display its own error. Verify recovery and actual1Hz/2Hz cadence under load/errors,including HAL-init failure,which currently logs and returns without automatic reinitialization. GPIO7 is polled once per loop; compare scoped short/held FAULT_N pulses with logged/displayed events because brief faults can be missed. Rationale: source-level error branches and500ms deadlines do not establish physical recovery or timing.
7. **Power and environment qualification.** At4.75/5.25V board input and0–40C non-condensing ambient,test continuous and transient simultaneous rail allocations222/472mA,rail3.0–3.6V,actual converter efficiency>=82% selection target,and total input<=250mA continuous/<=450mA peak including startup/recycle and upstream overhead. Use a reviewed load fixture that subtracts actual onboard draw to avoid double-loading the allocation; never force3V3 above its envelope. Capture current with bandwidth adequate for observed startup/burst edges,document bandwidth,burden and peak duration; a DMM average or a450mA fixture clamp does not prove the circuit's natural peak. Test radio loads separately using reviewed firmware; current application starts no radio and cannot qualify radio operation. Reconcile per-part leakage,support allowance,U5 dynamic/input-level currents,U6 combined2uA allowance,reset at shutdown/UVLO and retained charge. Rationale: nominal arithmetic and the approximately0.49A fault limit cannot prove the operating budget.
8. **Protection,RF and mechanical review.** Establish ESD test level/method before specialist testing,then verify TVS/eFuse overshoot,CC protection,short-fault/recovery and thermal behavior with controlled fixtures. Reconcile the15mm RF target with manufacturer guidance without waiving it; evaluate cable/enclosure/metal geometry and antenna keepouts. Inspect USB mating,thickness tolerance,header access,sensor airflow and thermal error despite>20mm geometric heat separation. Record results before any production decision. Rationale: clean DRC and separation distances do not qualify EMI,RF,ESD or sensing accuracy.
