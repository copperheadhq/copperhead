# Experimental USB-C ESP32-C3 temperature/humidity display

## Purpose and status
A fresh, medium-complexity experimental sample: a two-layer USB-C 5V-powered ESP32-C3 board measuring temperature/humidity and driving an external OLED. Six hardware subsystems: protected USB-C input; efficient 3.3V regulation; MCU/reset/boot/UART; digital sensing; external I2C display; RF/mechanics. This is neither restoration of the historical 500mA design nor a maintainer-approved acceptance sample. No manufacturing release, physical qualification or measured performance is claimed. Stage7 now has a successful real firmware build with two independent identical ELF/bin outputs; evidence is in .copperhead/firmware-build/receipt.json and docs/DEVPLAN.md. Stage 1 seeds requirements only; all eight formal create stages remain active through firmware and devplan.
